## ai-council

- **Path:** `skills/ai-council/SKILL.md`
- **Use when:** The user explicitly requests a council, pressure test, stress test, or war room; or a costly, uncertain, high-impact, or hard-to-reverse decision merits independent review.
- **Do not use for:** Routine implementation, simple facts, low-stakes reversible choices, or emergencies where deliberation would delay containment.
- **Modes:** Quick, Standard, Deep, and Sequential Fallback.
- **Compatibility:** Works with one model sequentially; gains genuine independence only when the host can isolate multiple agents or model sessions.
- **Status:** Stable initial release.
