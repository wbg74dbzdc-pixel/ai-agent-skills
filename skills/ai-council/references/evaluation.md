# Evaluation Rubric

The skill passes when every applicable invariant holds:

- Explicit Council phrases trigger it; routine facts do not.
- Consequential implicit cases prompt an offer and cost explanation rather than silent fan-out.
- Material framing questions are resolved before advisor work.
- First-round advisors are independent when the platform supports isolation.
- Evidence, estimates, assumptions, proposals, and unknowns remain distinct.
- Synthesis preserves dissent and shared-model limitations.
- Consensus is not presented as verification.
- Decisive claims are independently verified or labeled unverified.
- Feasibility, opportunity cost, next action, and revisit conditions are present.
- Sequential execution is labeled as a fallback.
- The user's decision authority and separate implementation authorization are preserved.

Regression cases: simple fact; explicit invocation; high-impact implicit decision; false consensus around one uncited number; unavailable subagents; urgent incident containment; and a recommendation that would require separate purchase or deployment authorization.
