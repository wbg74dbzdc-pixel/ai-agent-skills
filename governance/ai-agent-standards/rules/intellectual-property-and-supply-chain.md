# Intellectual Property and Supply Chain

- Record provenance, ownership, license, version, source, modification, attribution, and usage restrictions for code, packages, models, datasets, text, images, audio, video, fonts, trademarks, and generated assets.
- Confirm dependency and asset licenses are mutually compatible and compatible with the product's intended distribution and business model.
- Preserve required copyright notices, license texts, attributions, and source-offer obligations.
- Never assume that public availability, search-engine visibility, AI generation, or lack of a watermark grants usage rights.
- Scan dependencies for known vulnerabilities, abandoned packages, suspicious maintainers, typosquatting, and unnecessary transitive risk.
- Pin versions and verify integrity where practical; review lockfile and dependency changes.
- Produce and retain an SBOM when risk, regulation, customers, deployment context, or incident response needs justify it.
- Identify trademark, publicity, patent, dataset, model, and training-data questions that require qualified review rather than inventing clearance.
- Maintain a replacement or removal path for third-party components and assets.
