# Release Compliance

Explicitly assess which categories apply; do not silently skip the gate.

- Privacy notices, data inventory, lawful basis, consent, minimization, retention, deletion, export, and user rights.
- Authentication, authorization, secrets, secure defaults, dependency risk, abuse cases, monitoring, incident response, backup, and recovery.
- Terms, conditions, cancellation and refund rules, warranties, age restrictions, business identity, and required disclosures.
- Cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party embeds, processors, and cross-border transfers.
- Accessibility: semantics, labels, keyboard access, focus, contrast, alt text, captions, motion, errors, and assistive-technology testing.
- Copyright, licenses, trademarks, fonts, media, datasets, generated content, and attribution.
- Truthful product claims; no fake reviews, invented customers, dark patterns, or unsupported performance, health, safety, or financial claims.
- Platform, store, payment, export, industry, and applicable local legal requirements.
- Support, moderation, deletion, recovery, and operational ownership.
- Policy-to-product accuracy: notices, terms, permissions, disclosures, and consent interfaces must describe actual current behavior rather than generic boilerplate.
- Versioned acceptance and consent evidence where applicable, including a usable withdrawal path.
- AI-specific applicability, provider and data-flow documentation, transparency, human oversight, and appeal or correction paths where relevant.
- Marketing substantiation for explicit and implied claims, honest endorsements, material-relationship disclosures, and review integrity.
- Dependency and asset provenance, license compatibility, required notices, vulnerability status, and an SBOM when justified by risk or customer requirements.

Record jurisdiction, product facts, sources, owner, status, evidence, blockers, and review date. A checklist or AI-generated policy does not guarantee compliance. Escalate unresolved legal questions to a qualified professional.
