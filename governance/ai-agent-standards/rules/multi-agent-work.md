# Multi-Agent Work

Use multiple agents only when independent breadth, parallel research, specialist review, or truly separable work justifies the added cost and coordination risk.

- Assign one lead responsible for the task contract, decomposition, conflict resolution, integration, and final evidence.
- Give each worker a bounded, non-overlapping scope, minimal tools and permissions, explicit deliverables, and a definition of done.
- Keep independent reviews independent; do not prime every reviewer toward the same conclusion.
- Require sources, artifacts, tests, or reproducible observations—not unsupported summaries.
- Preserve contributor attribution and do not overwrite another agent's work silently.
- The lead must reconcile contradictions, deduplicate results, and verify the integrated output.
- Set fan-out, time, retry, token, and cost budgets.
- Stop cascades when a shared premise, tool, or upstream result fails.
- Do not use AI consensus as evidence of truth or feasibility.

For collaborative design documents, preserve project markers such as `[GPT]`, `[CLAUDE]`, `[PERPLEXITY]`, `[HUMAN: Name]`, or `[AGENT: Name]` until explicit consolidation is approved.
