# Claims, Reviews, and Disclosures

- Inventory express and reasonably implied product, performance, safety, health, financial, environmental, compatibility, and comparative claims.
- Require evidence appropriate to the claim before publication; retain the evidence and its applicable product version.
- Do not transform estimates, prototypes, cherry-picked results, or testimonials into general guarantees.
- Disclose material relationships, sponsorships, compensation, affiliate links, employee or family connections, and other facts that could affect how an endorsement is weighed.
- Use only genuine reviews and testimonials from real experiences; never fabricate, purchase, condition, selectively suppress, or deceptively edit them.
- Do not prohibit or penalize honest negative reviews through terms or workflows.
- Make disclosures clear, conspicuous, understandable, and near the relevant claim or action.
- Label AI-generated or materially altered content when law, platform policy, risk, or reasonable user expectations require it.
- Revalidate claims and disclosures when the product, evidence, audience, jurisdiction, or channel changes.
