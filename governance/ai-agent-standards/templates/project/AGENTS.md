This project adopts the public standards snapshot at `wbg74dbzdc-pixel/ai-agent-skills/governance/ai-agent-standards` at revision: `PIN_PUBLIC_SNAPSHOT_REVISION_HERE`.

Read the adopted universal constitution, then these project-specific rules. Project rules control within their scope but cannot silently weaken honesty, evidence, safety, privacy, preservation, or owner authority.

## End goal and product intent

State the product goal, users, and experience to preserve.

## Source of truth

Name authoritative specifications, project state, decision log, and precedence.

## Non-negotiables and rejected directions

List locked decisions and what requires owner approval to reopen.

## Architecture and implementation constraints

List technologies, boundaries, patterns, schema policy, performance budgets, and prohibited approaches.

## Authority

List allowed actions and actions requiring confirmation.

## Required validation

List build, test, lint, type-check, benchmark, visual, accessibility, security, and acceptance commands.

## Review and attribution

Define contributor markers, review stages, decision labels, final owner, and merge rules.

## Release gate

List product-specific privacy, security, accessibility, licensing, legal, platform, and operational checks.
