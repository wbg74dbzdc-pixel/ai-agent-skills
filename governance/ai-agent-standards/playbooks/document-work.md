# Document Work Playbook

1. Confirm audience, purpose, required format, authoritative input, and whether to add, revise, replace, redline, or preserve variants.
2. Extract and inspect the full source, including tables, notes, images, comments, and revision markers when relevant.
3. Preserve terminology, structure, provenance, exact questions, and requested existing content.
4. Mark AI contributions using the project's attribution scheme.
5. Validate internal consistency, references, figures, dates, and claims.
6. Render visual documents and inspect every page for clipping, overflow, broken tables, missing images, or unreadable styling.
7. Save to the authorized durable destination and verify the final artifact opens.

Never silently replace material when the owner asked to add to it.
