# Incident and User-Rights Response Playbook

## Prepare

- Name owners and contact paths for security incidents, privacy requests, accessibility complaints, moderation issues, intellectual-property claims, refunds, and legal escalation.
- Maintain data and vendor inventories, logging, backups, restoration tests, credential rotation, and current jurisdiction or platform requirements.
- Define safe identity verification that does not collect unnecessary additional data.

## Respond

1. Record receipt time, reporter, affected product, request or incident type, and known facts.
2. Preserve relevant evidence while restricting unnecessary access.
3. Contain active harm using the least destructive authorized action.
4. Determine affected systems, people, data, vendors, jurisdictions, contracts, and deadlines from authoritative current sources.
5. Fulfill access, correction, export, deletion, restriction, consent withdrawal, complaint, or appeal requests as applicable.
6. Coordinate required notices, vendor actions, support communication, and qualified legal review.
7. Verify the response or recovery and document what remains unresolved.
8. Correct root causes and add regression tests, monitoring, and updated procedures.

Do not invent universal response deadlines, expose another person's data, delete evidence, promise an outcome before verification, or conceal a material incident.
