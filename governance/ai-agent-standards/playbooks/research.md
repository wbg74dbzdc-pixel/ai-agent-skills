# Research Playbook

1. Define the decision the research must support, scope, freshness, and acceptable evidence.
2. Search broadly enough to discover the field, then prioritize authoritative primary sources.
3. Record source, date, version, jurisdiction, methodology, and relevant limitations.
4. Separate source claims from inference and recommendation.
5. Triangulate high-impact or surprising claims and investigate contradictions.
6. Search for counterevidence and failure cases.
7. State what is unknown and what would resolve it.
8. Cite claims near their support and avoid excessive quotation.
9. Finish with decision-relevant findings, confidence basis, risks, and next step.

Do not use popularity, search ranking, or AI consensus as a substitute for evidence.
