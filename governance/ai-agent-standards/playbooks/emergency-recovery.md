# Emergency Recovery Playbook

1. Stop expanding the incident and preserve evidence.
2. Identify affected users, systems, data, credentials, and external actions.
3. Verify authority before containment actions that are destructive or externally consequential.
4. Revoke or isolate compromised access, stop unsafe automation, and protect backups when authorized.
5. Restore from a known-good state through the safest reversible path.
6. Validate integrity, security, and critical behavior before resuming operation.
7. Record timeline, decisions, actions, evidence, remaining risk, and notifications required.
8. Add tests, controls, and playbook changes that prevent recurrence.

Do not destroy logs, conceal errors, improvise with unverified backups, or claim recovery until checks confirm it.
